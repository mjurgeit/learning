## 1. Chosen niche and why
**Audience: US-based freelancers / solo service providers** (designers, writers, developers, VAs, consultants) who get paid irregularly, owe quarterly estimated tax, and must chase invoices.
Options considered: freelancers, ADHD, creators, small-business finance. Freelancers won because:
- Size: 72.9M US freelance workers (MBO Partners 2025) to 76.4M (Upwork survey) [blog-claim via jobbers.io / smallbiztrends summaries]; BLS counts only ~9.8M primary independent workers [blog-claim]. Even the narrowest count is huge.
- Hard, recurring, money-shaped pains: 71% of freelancers had trouble collecting payment at some point and 81% of those were paid late [verified, Freelancers Union survey PDF, 2014 data - old]; 86.3% said income irregularity hurt mental health in 2024 [blog-claim]; self-employment tax is 15.3% on the first $176,100 of net earnings in 2026 [blog-claim via TaxAct/aggregator summary], and four estimated payments a year (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 18 2027) [verified, TaxAct 2026 dates page via search]. Money pain = willingness to pay.
- Demand evidence on Etsy: a "Notion Business & Life Planner / Freelance Template" at $49.99 shows 710 reviews and 18,594 favourites; a "Tax Deduction Tracker Excel for freelancers" at $4.49 shows 816 reviews [blog-claim, from a search-result summary of live listings; open the listings to confirm]. Several Freelancer finance Google-Sheets listings exist at $12.99-$16 [verified present in search results].
- Competition: "Notion template" has 50,000+ Etsy listings and generic "life OS" is saturated; profession-specific, money-focused systems remain open [blog-claim, kupkaike.com / unilink.us]. Median "notion template" Etsy price $12.66, 43% of listings under $10 [blog-claim]. So we do NOT sell a generic planner; we sell tax + invoice + profitability logic that generic templates lack.
- Margin: ~88% after Etsy fees on a $19.99 sale (section 5), zero fulfilment, no inventory.

**Angle / gap (our differentiators):**
1. **IRS estimated-tax periods, not calendar quarters** (Jan-Mar, Apr-May, Jun-Aug, Sep-Dec) - the tax estimator uses the real payment schedule. (Whether each competitor does this is [unverified]; check their previews.)
2. **Invoice auto-reconciliation**: log a payment with an invoice number and the invoice flips Paid/Partial/Overdue; no double entry.
3. **Effective hourly rate per project** and a rate calculator that works backwards from take-home pay - answers "was that client worth it?" Most finance sheets stop at income/expense.
4. **Same data model in Sheets and Notion**, sold individually or as a bundle. Buyers on either tool are served.
5. Every product ships with a **DEMO file** pre-filled with sample data and a blank file.

## 2. Customer personas
- **Maya, 29, freelance designer** (2-4 years in). Uses a notes app and bank statements. Dreads April and September. Pain: "How much of this payment is mine?" Buys the Finance Tracker ($19) and Rate Calculator ($9).
- **Dev, 36, contractor/consultant** with 3-6 clients on retainers. Wants Notion CRM + project hub. Buys Notion Freelancer OS ($24) and bundle.
- **Priya, 24, new side-hustler / VA** with a day job. Price-sensitive; starts with the $9-12 items, upsold to the starter bundle.
Problem solved: "Know what I earned, what I owe in tax, who owes me, and which work pays" in 10 minutes a week.

## 10. Risks, policy, kill criteria
**Risks:** (1) Notion/spreadsheet saturation and copycats [medium]; (2) tax-advice liability - mitigated by disclaimer, "estimator" wording, user-editable rates, and no claim of correctness [high importance]; (3) rate changes yearly (SE tax cap, mileage rate mid-2026 revision) - needs annual update [medium]; (4) Etsy dependency, fee changes, listing suppression; (5) piracy/sharing of files; (6) Notion link duplication ("Duplicate" link can be shared) - accept; watermarked license text.
**Platform rules:** Etsy Creativity Standards (July 2024; rewritten June 2025) allow AI-assisted digital items under "Designed by a seller", require an AI disclosure in the description [blog-claim from several summaries; read Etsy's current policy page before listing]. We write the logic/layout ourselves and use Claude as a tool; disclose it. Original work only - do not copy competitor layouts, thumbnails or text. Don't use Notion/Google/Microsoft logos as if endorsed; "works with Google Sheets" phrasing only. Tax statements: cite IRS, avoid promising savings. Mileage rate: IRS announced 72.5c/mile for 2026, reportedly revised to 76c from July 1 2026 [verified irs.gov newsroom for 72.5c; mid-year revision from ifebp.org summary - confirm at irs.gov].
**1099-K note:** thresholds for 2026 reported inconsistently ($20,000/200 transactions most-current vs older $600) [blog-claim]; we do not build thresholds into the products.
**Kill / pivot criteria:** after 90 days, if total visits < 1,500 or < 10 sales or conversion < 0.5% on all listings after two title/thumbnail rewrites -> stop paid effort, keep listings live (they cost cents). After 6 months if monthly gross < $300 with >=10 listings -> pivot to a different niche (e.g. ADHD / small-business finance) using the same file-building pipeline. Never spend more than $60 before first $100 of revenue.
