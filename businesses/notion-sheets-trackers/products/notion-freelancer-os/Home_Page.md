# Freelancer OS - Home

> Your clients, projects, invoices and taxes in one place. Start at "1. Start here" below.

## 1. Start here (5 minutes)
- [ ] Duplicate this template into your workspace
- [ ] Delete the rows marked (sample)
- [ ] Add your first client in Clients
- [ ] Add your first project and link the client
- [ ] Add your tax-period due dates in Tax Periods (check irs.gov)

## 2. Every day
- Today's tasks (linked view of Tasks, Due = today)
- Log time: update "Hours Logged" on the project when you stop work

## 3. Every week (Friday, 15 minutes)
- Money Log: log every payment and expense
- Invoices: send any pending invoice; chase everything with Days Overdue > 0
- Projects: any project with Hours Over Estimate > 0? Note why; price the next one higher

## 4. Every tax period
- Money Log filtered to the period: income minus deductible expenses = profit
- Move roughly 25-35% of profit to a tax savings account (adjust to your situation)
- Pay by the due date shown in Tax Periods

*Planning tool, not tax advice.*
