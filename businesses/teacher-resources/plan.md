## 1. Niche and why
**Chosen niche: Grade 3 math, one unit only at first: FRACTIONS (unit fractions, number lines, equivalence, comparing).** Dual-tagged to CCSS 3.NF and Texas TEKS Grade 3 fraction standards. Print-and-go, black-and-white friendly, every product ships with an answer key.

Why:
- Grade 3 is where fractions are introduced, so every 3rd-grade teacher teaches the unit once a year, with a predictable buying window (roughly Jan-Mar and again around Sept-Oct, [estimate], no seasonality data was found).
- Demand evidence: dozens of competing fractions task-card bundles on TPT priced $39-$94 [blog-claim, search snippets], and 5,000+ elementary-math items on Etsy [blog-claim]. Buyer intent is proven; this is not a speculative category.
- Standards are specific and checkable: CCSS 3.NF.A.1 (fraction 1/b as one of b equal parts), 3.NF.A.2 (number line), 3.NF.A.3b/c/d (equivalent, whole numbers as fractions, compare) [verified via search of standards text]. Texas TEKS 3.3A (represent fractions, denominators 2,3,4,6,8), 3.3B (number line), 3.3D (compose/decompose), 3.3F (equivalent) [blog-claim from vendor alignment pages; Texas has revised/renumbered TEKS, so verify against current TEA documents before printing numbers on the product. The PDFs already carry a disclaimer].
- Competition level: HIGH on TPT (many veteran stores, 7M+ products, 100k+ sellers per a blog), MEDIUM on Etsy where teacher resources are mixed with homeschool printables. Generic "math worksheets" is hopeless; the tight unit-level niche is the only viable entry.
- Gap/angle: (1) one unit, finished, dual CCSS+TEKS (many TPT stores are CCSS-only or Texas-only); (2) low price point per component with a cheap full bundle, versus $39-$94 mega-bundles that are overkill for a 2-week unit; (3) strictly no-prep, ink-light, answer keys on every file; (4) all art is vector drawn by script, so no clipart licensing chain to break.

Honest caveat: AI/free-worksheet pressure is real (Education.com, Twinkl and others give 3rd-grade fractions worksheets away). Podcasts in the TPT community advise avoiding "simple worksheets that AI can clone" [blog-claim]. Our differentiation must be standards precision, pedagogy (visual models, number lines), and bundles, not raw worksheets.

## 10. Risks, policy rules, kill criteria
- **Copyright/IP**: TPT prohibits listing infringing material [blog-claim, CEO statement via EdWeek]. Our files contain no clipart/fonts (reportlab vector + built-in Helvetica). If you add clipart or fonts, confirm a commercial license covering TPT/Etsy products and give required credit. Do not copy competitor worksheets or textbook (Go Math, Eureka) pages; use CCSS/TEKS standard numbers and short descriptors only, and keep the "not affiliated" statement. Never use trademarked characters (Pokemon, Disney) in themes.
- **Standards claims**: CCSS text is public, but claim "aligned", not "endorsed"; re-verify the TEKS numbering (Texas revised TEKS; my search showed 3.3A/B/D/F but also vendor pages citing 3.7A for number lines, so numbering is inconsistent across sources).
- **AI disclosure**: Etsy requires the "Designed by seller" attribute and honest creation disclosure for AI-involved items [blog-claim, consistent with market report]. I found NO official TPT AI policy in search; check TPT's terms before upload. This product's art is code-generated and its text templated, with answers computed programmatically, but state "created with the help of AI tools and reviewed by the seller" to be safe.
- **Fee/policy change**; **saturation/free competition**; **quality**: a wrong answer key destroys reviews, so every key must be hand-checked.
- **Tax/ID**: sales tax/VAT on digital goods and payout identity verification are human tasks.
- **Kill criteria**: at day 90, if total across both platforms is under $30 gross AND under 100 product views/week, stop new building and pivot niche (e.g., KDP or Canva templates). At month 6, if under $100/month with 18+ listings and 15+ hrs spent on Pinterest, cut to maintenance. Continue only if trailing 3-month revenue is rising.
