### 1.3 Evidence for realtors
- 1,400,000 NAR members in 2024 and 2025; median gross income $58,100 in 2024; typical member 10 transactions [verified, NAR 2025 Member Profile via search results]. Most agents are independent contractors who must buy their own marketing, so they are small-business buyers, not consumers.
- Social media = 75% of agents' tech use and the top quality-lead source (39%); 34% spend $50-250/mo on tech tools, 20% $251-500, 24% over $500 [verified, NAR 2025 Technology Survey via search results]. They already pay recurring money for tools; a one-time $19-39 template is a small ticket.
- Etsy proof of real sales: SmartAgentDocs 6,392 sales/5.0 stars/572 reviews (earlier snippet said 7,484, both since 2025; the count moved) ; RealEstatePromo 3,237 sales since 2021, 378 reviews; StructuredCreativeCo 1,878; AletaCreative 1,292; AgentCollective 1,100 (since 2022); EstateElements 234 [all verified-snippet]. A shop reaching ~6-7k sales within about a year shows the category converts.
- Price ladder is higher than generic Canva: $2.24 open-house sign-in to $35 (was $50) listing/buyer presentation bundle; other bundles $18.99-$39.90; $39 Highnote buyer presentation [verified-snippet]. Generic Canva median is $4.
- Regulatory tailwind: since Aug 2024 NAR settlement, a written buyer representation agreement is required before touring; Texas made written buyer agreements law statewide effective 2026-01-01 [verified, state/association pages via search]. Agents must now *explain compensation and value* in a consultation. This is a recurring new need (new agents, new state rules) not yet saturated with polished, plain-English visuals.

### 1.4 Our gap/angle
Competitors sell either (a) generic Instagram post packs or (b) big long presentations. Few are positioned around the **post-settlement buyer consultation**. Our angle: **"The Buyer Consultation Kit"**: a short (12-16 page) buyer presentation, a commission/value conversation guide, an agent-agreement explainer handout (visual explainer only, not a legal contract), plus follow-up templates (post-showing, open house). Written in plain English, state-neutral, with fill-in blanks. Sell small starter products ($7-12) for ranking/reviews, hero bundles ($29-49), and an "All-Year Agent Toolkit" ($79 on Payhip).

Not legal advice: we never supply contract forms. Every listing carries the line "Educational/marketing template; not legal advice or a contract; check your brokerage and state rules."

## 2. Personas
1. **Newer agent (0-3 yrs), "Jess, 29":** brokerage gives no brand kit; income below median; spends evenings in Canva. Pain: looks amateur, doesn't know what to say in a buyer consult. Budget $10-40 one-off.
2. **Mid-career solo agent, "Dave, 46":** 10+ deals/yr; was winging buyer conversations; the settlement forced him to defend his commission. Wants a ready deck and handouts by tomorrow. Budget $30-60.
3. **Team admin/marketing assistant:** buys bundles for several agents; values consistent kit (colors editable). 

Problem solved: save 5-15 hours of design/copywriting; look professional; have compliant-sounding, consumer-friendly language for new buyer-agency conversations.

## 10. Traffic plan
1. **Etsy SEO (primary):** 13 tags, front-load keyword, attributes filled, 10 photos incl. video mockup, renew via Etsy ads at $1/day only after a listing has favorites >5.
2. **Pinterest:** realtors search for Instagram ideas and checklists; 10 pins per listing (various mockups), 3-5 pins/day via manual scheduling; pins link to Etsy listing/our Payhip product page. Pinterest is allowed to link to Etsy.
3. **Payhip:** own storefront captures non-Etsy buyers; Payhip is not linked from Etsy listings (see 10.2).
4. **Communities:** value-first posts in realtor Facebook groups and r/realtors (check each group's self-promo rules); sample freebie (open-house sign-in) as lead magnet on Pinterest/Payhip email list.
5. **YouTube Shorts/Instagram:** 30-sec "how to edit in Canva" demos [estimate: slow ramp].

### 10.2 Etsy off-platform rule
Etsy prohibits diverting buyers off-platform to complete a purchase; other-venue links are clickable only on the About page [verified, Etsy House Rules via search]. So Etsy delivery PDFs must not advertise Payhip discounts. Keep the two storefronts separate (Payhip fed by Pinterest/email).

## 11. Risks, policies, kill criteria
**Risks**
1. Competitors with 1-7k sales and reviews outrank us; mitigate with narrow long-tail and unique consult kit.
2. Etsy search shifts / views stall; mitigate via Pinterest/Payhip.
3. Legal/compliance: agent agreements differ by state; real estate advertising rules (brokerage name, fair housing, license number). Mitigate: no contract language, disclaimer, "Equal Housing" placeholder mentions in specs, no claims of NAR endorsement or use of the REALTOR(R) trademark in a way implying affiliation (use "real estate agent" in titles; "realtor" as a generic search keyword is common on Etsy but avoid the (R) logo/NAR logo).
4. Canva licensing: use only original layouts; Pro elements only via template link; no standalone stock; no selling flattened files containing Pro content [Canva help via search].
5. Etsy AI policy (Creativity Standards): AI-assisted designs must be disclosed; items listed "Designed by seller"; 17,000+ listings reportedly removed early 2025 [blog-claim]. We disclose AI-assisted copy drafting in descriptions.
6. IP: no copying competitor layouts/copy; no brokerage logos (Compass, RE/MAX etc.) or MLS marks.
7. Link-based delivery: Canva link can change or be broken; keep a backup template copy and monitor link validity.
8. Market risk: housing slowdown reduces agent count/spend (NAR median income only $58.1k).

**Kill/pivot criteria**
- After 90 days with 25+ listings: fewer than 10 total sales AND <2,000 total listing views -> pivot to therapists (Plan B) using the same system.
- After 6 months: <$150/month gross with 40 listings -> stop new production; maintain only.
- Any Etsy shop suspension or Canva account strike -> stop and review policy before continuing.
- Positive signal: >=1 sale per 100 views by day 60 -> double down (add 20 listings).

Plan B niche: therapists/coaches (workbook/handout Canva templates), reusing the build pipeline.
