Dimensions: Presentation 16:9, 1920 x 1080 px (Canva "Presentation (16:9)"). Brand: see brand-system.md.
Purpose: a presentation an agent walks a new buyer through in their first meeting; covers process, how the agent is paid, and the written buyer agreement in plain English. NOT a contract. Disclaimers on p.16.
Fill-ins are [IN BRACKETS]. State-neutral wording; agents adapt to their MLS/state.
