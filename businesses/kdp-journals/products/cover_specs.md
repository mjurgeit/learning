## Brief 1: Midlife Reset (journal)
Mood: calm, warm, grown-up, not clinical and not "pink and cute". Palette: warm cream background (#F6EFE6), deep teal (#1F5C5C), terracotta accent (#C9704F). Type: serif title (e.g. Playfair Display / Cormorant, free Google Fonts), clean sans subtitle. Imagery: a simple botanical line drawing (eucalyptus or olive branch) drawn or generated from original/licensed art, or a typographic-only cover. No stock photos of women (licensing/likeness risk, and saturated look). Front: title "Midlife Reset", subtitle line, small "90 days" badge. Back: 3 bullets from the description, one-line disclaimer ("not medical advice"), barcode space. If you use AI-generated art, tick the AI-generated content box in KDP.

## Brief 2: The Gardener's Large Print Word Search
Mood: friendly, bold, readable at thumbnail size (Amazon thumbnails are tiny). Palette: leaf green (#2F6B3A), cream (#FAF5E6), sunflower yellow (#F2B705). Big title in heavy serif, at least 40 percent of cover height. Imagery: flat-style vegetables/flowers/watering can illustrated in a border; show a small word-search grid motif. Put "LARGE PRINT" and "60 Puzzles" in a high-contrast banner. Back: 4 bullets, sample of 6 words, barcode space.
