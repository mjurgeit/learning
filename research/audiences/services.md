## Ranking and the best 2

1. **Contractors / trades (27)**: owner-operators spend business money on estimating and contract paperwork, and a bad bid or missing lien waiver costs real dollars.
2. **Insurance agents (27)**: highest recurrence (renewals, cross-sell, CE) and a regulated, error-costly environment. Evidence of paying is thinner than for trades (see caveats).
3. Bookkeepers (25): strong pain and price, but AI and vertical tools (QuickBooks, etc.) are eating the entry-level work.
4. Photographers (21): best reachability, but contract and client-guide bundles are saturated and cheap.
5-6. Wedding planners, trainers (20). 7-8. Cleaning, freelancers (19). 9. VAs (17).

**Named best 2: contractors/trades and insurance agents.** Both fit the "business money, costly errors" lesson. Both are also the same kind of buyer as the realtor kit that report 10 already picks (independent, commission or job-based, higher price tolerance), so any traffic and positioning learning carries over.

## Product ideas (best 2)

**Contractors / trades** ($39-99; $149 for full-trade bundle)
1. Single-trade estimating workbook (e.g. HVAC install or roofing) with flat-rate price book, markup and margin check, customer quote PDF, in Excel and Google Sheets.
2. State-specific lien-waiver and change-order pack (start with 2-3 states, e.g. TX, FL, CA), with "last verified" dates.
3. Job-costing and cash-flow tracker plus bid-vs-actual dashboard (shows when jobs lost money).

**Insurance agents** ($29-79; $149 bundle)
1. Annual renewal-retention system: 60/30/7-day outreach scripts, tracker, review-call agenda.
2. Medicare AEP/OEP calendar and compliance-aware client communication kit (seasonal, resold each year).
3. New-agency startup and CE/licence-tracking workbook, plus referral-partner (realtor, mortgage) outreach kit.

## Compliance risks

- Contractors: lien waiver and contract forms are state-specific legal documents; wrong form can be invalid. Needs a "not legal advice" disclaimer, state labelling, dated updates, ideally a lawyer review. Licence rules vary by state.
- Insurance: client-facing marketing and Medicare materials are regulated (CMS marketing rules, state advertising/filing rules, TCPA for outreach scripts). Sell neutral editable frameworks, not "approved" scripts, and state that the agent is responsible for compliance. Avoid carrier trademarks.
- Both: do not claim guaranteed earnings or savings.

## Caveats

- Price evidence for insurance agents is the weakest of the top group (no prices seen). Validate with a cheap test (one listing, one Big I-adjacent FB group) before building more.
- Bookkeepers could outrank insurance agents if a bookkeeper-specific search showed paying template buyers; I did not check prices or competitors there.
- All headcounts are jobs (employees included), not business owners; reachable buyer count is lower.
