## Best 2

1. **Daycare / preschool owners (29).** Real business money, compliance-driven (state licensing, ratios, parent handbooks, incident forms), annual renewals, and no tool-spend or template-seller saturation found. Weakest link: no spend evidence found, and many states supply free official templates.
2. **Small restaurants / cafes / food trucks (26)**, framed as state/county-specific food-safety and permit packs (health-inspection prep, temperature/cleaning logs, mobile-vendor permit checklists). Chosen over the nonprofit/church bucket on price power (business money, inspection failure costs real dollars). Nonprofits/churches tie at 26 and are the first alternate, especially a child-safety/volunteer-screening policy kit (overlaps with the daycare buyer).

Not recommended as a primary pick: sellers/creators/authors/homeschool. Reasons below.

## Per-audience evidence

### 1. Daycare / preschool owners
- Size: CCAoA counted 92,786 licensed child care centers (41 states reporting) and 94,227 licensed family child care homes (39 states) in 2023; FCC homes down 12% since 2019. [snippet of Child Care Aware / FFYF summary] https://www.ffyf.org/resources/2025/05/new-resource-reveals-notable-changes-in-price-and-supply-of-child-care and https://www.childcareaware.org/thechildcarestandstill. So roughly 190k licensed providers in reporting states [estimate: sum, states not fully covered, so a floor]. BLS: 555,100 preschool teachers, 991,600 childcare workers (these are employees, not owners) [primary, via 07]. Supply is shrinking, a mild negative.
- Money: owners run businesses with tuition revenue; no direct template or tool-spend figure found. Existing paid ecosystem: Vanco (payments) and DailyConnect (app) publish handbook guides and a free .docx, i.e. vendors give handbooks away as lead magnets [snippet] https://www.vancopayments.com/child-care/blog/childcare-parent-handbook , https://news.dailyconnect.com/daycare-parent-handbook-guide/. That caps price for a bare handbook; state-specific compliance value must be the differentiator. Typical price I'd expect $15-60 [estimate, unverified].
- Pain: licensing, parent policies, incident and medication forms, emergency plans. Errors cost a license or a lawsuit. Free state examples exist (Mass.gov parent handbook, Child Care Aware FCC handbook PDF) [snippet] https://www.mass.gov/doc/parent-handbook-3/download , https://www.childcareaware.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Family-Child-Care-Home_R10_ParentHandbook.pdf.
- Recurrence: annual license renewal, yearly fee/policy updates, new-enrolment packets each fall. 
- Reach: Facebook groups for home daycare providers, Pinterest, state associations, Etsy "daycare forms" searches (not verified in this run).
- Low-AI: ChatGPT can draft a handbook, but state-rule accuracy and licensing citations are the moat. 3.
- Gap: Etsy daycare printable listings exist but I did not inspect them; I scored 4 as unverified [estimate].

### 2. Small restaurants / cafes / food trucks
- Size: more than 1 million US restaurant/foodservice outlets by NRA (2020 study), ~731k restaurant and drinking-place establishments with employees; ~58,000 food trucks (IBISWorld via a snippet) [blog-claim/snippet] https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/how-many-restaurants-are-in-the-us , https://www.duck-hub.com/blog/restaurant-industry-statistics.
- Money: sector $1.5T sales projected 2025 [snippet, NRA 2025 report via search]. Template prices found: Payhip and Ko-fi HACCP templates for home bakers, caterers, food trucks exist (prices not seen) [snippet] https://payhip.com/b/OjCFD , https://ko-fi.com/s/588d11e118. Vendors FoodReady and SafetyCulture publish free HACCP templates and checklists [snippet] https://foodready.ai/?p=88249 , https://safetyculture.com/checklists/haccp/ (free competition lowers willingness to pay for generic logs).
- Pain: health inspections, HACCP plans required for some processes (reduced-oxygen packaging, etc.), permits per county. Fines/closure risk.
- Recurrence: daily logs, annual permit renewals, seasonal truck permits.
- Reach: food-truck Facebook groups, local commissary communities, Reddit r/foodtrucks (not checked), SBA/chamber events.
- Low-AI: generic templates are free everywhere, AI drafts plans fine; moat is jurisdiction-specific requirements (state/county) and fillable inspection-prep that matches the local form. 3.
- Gap: HACCP templates sold on Payhip/Ko-fi, so not empty; per-state packs unverified.

### 3. Small nonprofits and churches
- Size: 1.5M IRS-registered nonprofits (2016) and an estimated 2.5M+ counting congregations and grassroots groups [snippet; 2016 data] https://nonprofitpro.com/article/9-things-you-might-not-know-about-u-s-nonprofits , https://nonprofit.indiana.edu/our-focus/nonprofit-sector.html.
- Money: budgets are small; treasurers buy $10-40 templates occasionally [estimate]. Grant tracker templates already on Etsy at low prices and as free ClickUp/Smartsheet/Baserow templates [snippet, not opened] https://www.etsy.com/listing/1799831298 , https://www.smartsheet.com/content/grant-tracking-templates , https://baserow.io/templates/nonprofit-grant-tracker. Church volunteer forms are free from Typeform/Paperform/ClickUp [snippet] https://www.typeform.com/templates/t/church-volunteer-form-template.
- Pain: Form 990 deadlines, grant reporting, volunteer screening/child safety. Medium-high.
- Recurrence: annual (990, budget, grant cycles, VBS and event seasons).
- Reach: churches via denominational groups and Facebook admin groups; nonprofits via local associations; free-tool competition is dense.
- Low-AI: 3; grant narrative is AI-friendly, compliance forms less.

### 4. E-commerce bookkeeping (Etsy/Shopify/Amazon seller books)
- Size: Etsy 5.6M active sellers at end-2025, 5.706M in Q2 2026 per a snippet [primary-via-snippet/snippet] https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm (from 06), https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20251107/2-etsys-marketplace-has-how-many-sellers. Amazon and Shopify seller counts not searched.
- Money: Etsy mean ~$156/month gross, median est. $25-50/month [estimate from 06]. Sellers with that income hesitate to pay; the higher-volume sellers (the 1099-K group) pay.
- Pain: 1099-K threshold for 2025 is >$20,000 AND >200 transactions per the snippet (restored by the July 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill Act) [snippet, blog-claim] https://linkmybooks.com/blog/etsy-tax-forms , https://craftybase.com/blog/etsy-1099k; the 1099-K shows gross, not profit; Etsy-collected sales tax isn't on it.
- Recurrence: monthly/annual.
- Competition: Craftybase (inventory/COGS app) and LinkMyBooks (Etsy accounting) publish tax guides [snippet, not opened]; Beancount.io; accountants. SaaS dominates; spreadsheets are the low end.
- Low-AI: Etsy CSV to ChatGPT works; 2.

### 6. Homeschool parents
- Size: about 1.9M kids (3.4% of school-age children, 2022-23) [primary, NCES via Pew, from 07]; ~0.95M households [estimate, 07].
- Money: HSLDA poll about $1,295 per elementary student and $1,636 per older student yearly [survey, secondary, from 07]. Share going to digital planners unknown.

## Product ideas (3 each for the top picks, then one-liners)

**Daycare / preschool ($19-79 per pack, state-specific)**
1. State licensing "inspection-ready binder" for home child care (enrolment, immunization, emergency, incident, medication forms) built per state; start with one state with public rules.
2. Editable parent handbook + tuition/late-pickup/illness policy pack with state-rule citations and an annual update.
3. Staff onboarding and training-hours tracker with expiry reminders (CPR/first-aid, background-check dates).

**Restaurants / food trucks ($19-69)**
1. Mobile food vendor permit and commissary checklist pack per state/county.
2. Health-inspection self-audit + daily temperature/cleaning logs (fillable PDF + Sheets) aligned to FDA Food Code sections.
3. HACCP plan starter for specific processes (cold brew, sous-vide, house sauces) that need variance paperwork.

**Nonprofits/churches ($15-49):** child-safety/volunteer screening policy kit; grant calendar with funder-report tracker; event/VBS planner and budget.
**Sellers/bookkeeping ($10-29):** 1099-K reconciliation workbook; per-platform fee and COGS tracker; Etsy policies/returns/shipping template set.
**Homeschool ($6-15), creators ($9-29), authors ($9-29):** commodity bundles; not recommended.

## Compliance risks
- Daycare and food: state rules differ and change; a wrong "inspection-ready" claim exposes the seller. Need disclaimers, version dates, "not legal advice", and clearly state coverage; do not claim approval by any licensing body.
- Food: do not copy FDA Food Code text beyond fair use; cottage-food and mobile vendor laws vary by county.
- Nonprofits: no tax or legal advice (990, 501(c)(3) status); background-check forms touch FCRA rules.
- Seller/bookkeeping: tax content is advice-adjacent (1099-K thresholds shifted; the $20,000/200 figure is from snippet blogs, verify with IRS before use).
- Marketplace: Etsy digital-goods policies, trademark use of "Etsy" in branding kits, Canva template licensing for resale.

## Section: "sell picks and shovels to people who sell digital products"

Evidence of spending (all snippet-level, unopened):
- Etsy SEO/research tools run $5.50-$29.99/month. eRank claims 2M+ sellers; Alura 820,000+ sellers and 121,000 active users; EverBee Chrome extension 200,000 users [blog-claim/vendor claim, search snippet] https://revenuegeeks.com/etsy/seller-tools , https://www.alura.io/vs/everbee , https://revenuegeeks.com/compare/erank-vs-everbee. Vendor-claimed registered users are not paying users; all three have free tiers.
- Authors: Vellum $199.99-$249.99 and Atticus $147 one-time are real one-off purchases [blog-claim].
- Etsy branding kits sell at $6; Canva template bundles $20-40 [snippet]. Seller-facing templates are priced low because the buyers are poor on average (Etsy median est. $25-50/month gross, 06).
- Etsy 5.6M active sellers, flat/falling, many sellers per buyer (06).

Assessment: demand is real but price-limited and crowded with VC-backed tools and free tiers. A template seller cannot beat SaaS on features. Sellers who earn enough to pay are the 1099-K-and-above minority, and there bookkeeping/tax tooling is the opening, still occupied by Craftybase/LinkMyBooks. Compared with daycare and food-business owners, whose spend comes from business revenue and compliance fear, the seller audience ranks 4th-5th. Use it as a cross-sell, not a lead audience. Also note: this is the market where my own shop would compete for the same buyers it targets.
