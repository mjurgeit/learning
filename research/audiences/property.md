## Best 2
1. **Small landlords (28).** Biggest real pool, spend rent income (business money), costly legal errors, recurring turnover/notice/tax cycles, and AI/free tools only partly cover state-specific accuracy.
2. **Property managers (25)**, sold as the "multi-unit upgrade" of the landlord kit (same forms/checklists, more volume). Caveat: the pool is smaller and they mostly buy software. Investors and agents are close behind; agents are the weakest on gap (Canva bundles at $6-20 are saturated).

## Evidence by audience

### 1. Small landlords (28)
- **Size 5.** Of 49.5M US rental units, about 46% are in 1-4 unit properties; about 70% of rental properties (38% of units) are owned by individual investors [primary, Census/HUD Rental Housing Finance Survey 2021, via snippet] https://archives.hud.gov/news/2022/pr22-242.cfm ; https://alta.org/news-and-publications/news/20221206-Survey-Nearly-Half-of-Rental-Units-are-in-Properties-with-Four-or-Fewer-Units . A clean headcount of landlords (owners, not properties) was not found; the snippet's "15.9 million" figure is ambiguous (properties vs owners). Directionally millions.
- **Money 4.** They pay for forms: TurboTenant charges $79 per state lease in its free tier; Premium is $149/yr and includes a 32-form pack [snippet, vendor] https://www.turbotenant.com/lease-agreements-4-0/ . Etsy-type bundles price lower (exact prices not read). Money is rental income, so it is business spend [estimate].
- **Pain 5.** Notices, evictions and leases are state-specific; a wrong notice can void an eviction [estimate; no primary legal source opened].
- **Recurrence 4.** Each turnover needs application, lease, move-in checklist, notices; annual rent increases; year-end expense tracking [estimate].
- **Reach 4.** Search terms "landlord forms", "lease agreement template", "eviction notice template" return many listings (demand exists) [snippet]; communities (BiggerPockets forums, r/landlord, local landlord associations) not opened [estimate].
- **Low AI 3.** ChatGPT drafts a lease, but state-compliant, updated notices are valued, and free competitors (TurboTenant, Zillow Rental Manager: leases for 36 states, free e-sign) exist [snippet] https://www.turbotenant.com/reviews/turbotenant-vs-zillow-rental-manager/ .
- **Gap 3.** Etsy has multiple sellers (listed below), but no snippet showed a state-by-state, update-subscription format [estimate; unverified].

## Product ideas (top 2 audiences)
Small landlords / property managers:
1. **State-specific landlord notice pack** (late rent, entry, rent increase, non-renewal) for 1-2 states first, with a yearly update; $19-$39 per state [estimate; compare TurboTenant $79/lease].
2. **Turnover and move-in/move-out system** (inspection checklist with photo log, deposit-deduction letter, tenant communication scripts); $15-$29.
3. **Landlord tax-time tracker** (Schedule E categories, per-unit P&L, maintenance log, depreciation notes) in Sheets/Excel; $15-$25 or $9/yr refresh.
Optional PM tier: SOP binder and owner-report templates, $49-$99 [estimate].

## Compliance risks
- **Landlord/PM forms:** legal-form content can be seen as unauthorized practice of law or as wrong advice if a state rule changes; need clear "not legal advice", state-by-state review, dates and update policy. Fair Housing wording in tenant screening and ads; deposit and notice rules vary by state/city [estimate; no legal source checked].
- **Loan officers:** advertising needs NMLS ID and rule compliance; a template that omits it creates liability [estimate].
- **STR:** local permits/tax rules change by city; do not claim compliance coverage.
- **Agents:** MLS/brokerage-mandated forms; Fair Housing wording [estimate].
- **Platforms:** Etsy legal-template listings can be flagged as legal advice (not verified). Do not copy competitors' text.
