# Audience scoring rubric (shared)

Goal: find the audience where a small seller of niche digital products (templates, trackers, kits, possibly a later AI add-on) is most likely to earn real money. Read research/06-10 first (don't edit them). Key lessons so far: teachers and nurses buy at $3-6; AI tools already serve teachers free; generic products are being commoditized by AI; buyers who spend BUSINESS money pay more.

Score each audience 1-5 on each criterion, with the evidence and source URL (tag [primary], [survey], [blog-claim], [snippet], [estimate]):
1. Size: reachable headcount (US first)
2. Money: do they spend business money on tools/templates? Typical price they pay for similar products
3. Pain: legal/financial/compliance/time-critical tasks where errors are costly (accuracy has value)
4. Recurrence: need repeats weekly/monthly/yearly (renewals, updates, subscriptions)
5. Reachability: identifiable communities, search terms, Facebook groups, Pinterest/YouTube/LinkedIn, associations, conferences
6. Low AI substitution: how hard is it for them to get this free from ChatGPT/Canva/Notion AI or an existing vertical AI tool? (5 = hard to replace)
7. Competition gap: existing sellers saturate? (5 = lots of room)
Total /35. Also list: 3 concrete product ideas, price range, 2-3 named competitors (with URLs if found in search results; say if not opened), compliance risks.
Marketplaces/gov sites may be blocked; use search snippets and say so. Never invent figures or URLs. Tag every figure. Do not git commit.
