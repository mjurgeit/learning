## Verdict
- **One new shop as an income plan: no-go.** The data says a typical new seller earns little and the realistic year-1 hourly rate is about $1–3.
- **A small, capped experiment with a kill switch: go.** Upside exists but is concentrated in a few shops, so we only keep running what proves itself.
- **Effort is only justified by real signals,** not by category-level market size.

## Recommended plan (capped at ~$100 and ~60 hours total)
1. **Realtor buyer-consultation kit** (strongest buyer data, higher price). Grow to 25–40 pages and add a seller deck first. Fix the cut-off slide title.
2. **Freelancer finance tracker** (already built; broad audience).
3. Optional: **speech/OT therapist data-sheet pack** as the narrow side-line test.
4. Drop for now: TPT fractions, KDP perimenopause journal, generic planners.

## 30-day test per product
- Cost under $30; list 5–10 listings.
- **Continue** if ≥10 paid orders and ≥25 free-sample signups, no takedowns.
- **Stop or rework** if <5 orders and <10 signups.
- Record price, orders, views and conversion each week.
