# Research brief (shared by all business agents)

Context: repo `learning` is a "Digital Business Lab". Goal: run several small digital-product businesses (Etsy, Amazon KDP, Gumroad/Payhip, TPT) that earn real money. Read `README.md` and `research/01-market-report.md` first (do not edit them).

Today is 2026-10-03. Use WebSearch/WebFetch (load via ToolSearch `select:WebSearch,WebFetch` if not callable). Do deep research: run 15+ searches/fetches, prefer primary or data-backed sources, cross-check numbers, and mark every figure as [verified], [blog-claim] or [estimate]. Never invent data, sales numbers, or URLs. If something can't be verified, say so.

Write ONLY inside your assigned folder. Do not git commit. Required files in your folder:
1. `plan.md` – a DEFINITE business plan, not a mention:
   - Chosen niche(s) and why (demand evidence, competition level, gap/angle to differentiate)
   - Customer persona(s) and what problem the product solves
   - Product line: exact first 10 products (titles, formats, price) + bundle/upsell ladder
   - Competitor teardown: 5+ real competing shops/products found by research, their price, strengths, weaknesses, what we do better
   - Keyword/SEO list (20+ search terms, with how you found them) and listing title/tag/description templates
   - Pricing strategy & unit economics (fees by platform, margin per sale)
   - Revenue model: conservative / base / optimistic monthly revenue at months 1, 3, 6, 12, with explicit assumptions (traffic, conversion, price, listings)
   - 90-day launch calendar (week by week), time required per week, startup cost
   - Traffic plan (platform SEO, Pinterest, etc.) 
   - Risks, platform-policy/AI-disclosure/IP rules, and kill criteria (when to stop)
2. `skills.md` – exact skills/tools needed to execute (design, SEO, tools with free options and costs), what Claude can do for the owner vs. what the human must do (accounts, ID verification, payouts, uploading if no API), and a learning path.
3. `products/` – start actually producing: at least 3 ready-to-edit sample product assets or complete content (e.g. listing copy files, template specs, worksheets as markdown/HTML/CSV/PDF generated by script) so the owner can publish quickly. Original work only; no copying competitors.
4. `listings.md` – full ready-to-paste listing copy (title, 13 Etsy tags or platform equivalent, description, FAQ) for the first 5 products.

Finish with a short summary (under 200 words): niche chosen, best-case/base-case revenue, top 3 risks, and files created.
