## 3. Model

**Formula:** Serviceable market (SAM, $/yr) = headcount × share who buy digital templates × price per order × orders per buyer per year. Shop revenue = SAM × capture share.

**Assumptions (all [estimate], chosen by me, not measured):**
- Buy share (low/mid/high): teachers 10/25/40%; homeschool 15/30/50%; SLP 10/20/35%; OT, ABA 8/15/30%; realtors 5/10/20%; daycare, tutors/coaches 5/10/20% (daycare 5/10/20, tutors 5/10/20); nurses 2/5/10%; freelancers 1/3/6%. Teachers and homeschoolers get higher shares because of the TPT evidence; freelancers and nurses get low shares because the pools are broad and no template-buying evidence was found.
- Price per order: teachers $5 (87.9% of TPT items are under $5); homeschool and daycare $8; nurses $9; SLP/OT/ABA $12; freelancers $25; tutors/coaches $20; realtors $30. Prices follow the pricing already assumed in `03-ranking-and-launch-order.md` and `01-market-report.md` ($15-47 for templates; realtor kits $25-50).
- Orders per buyer per year (low/mid/high): teachers 3/6/10; homeschool 2/4/6; everyone else 1/2/3.
- Headcounts: teachers 3.2M; nurses 3.391M plus 267,889 BSN students; SLP 212,864; OT 169,736; ABA 327,675; realtors 1,453,690; freelancers 27.6M full-time only; daycare 555,100 preschool teachers plus 91,200 home providers; homeschool 0.95M households; tutors/coaches 174,660 plus 122,974 (worldwide, so an overcount for the US).
- Capture share of SAM for a new single shop (low/mid/high): 0.05% / 0.2% / 1%. [estimate] Same for all audiences so rankings stay comparable.

**Reality check on the capture column.** The high case is a theoretical ceiling, not a plan; for the big pools it implies six or seven figures from one shop, which the sources do not support. The only seller-income anchors are weak [blog-claim]: Etsy digital sellers median about £400-800/month (about $6-12k/yr at roughly 1.25-1.5 $/£, my conversion) and TPT where about half of sellers earn under $20/month and 81% of sales go to the top 1% ([survey], https://spencergreenhalgh.com/research/2020-koehler-et-al-tpt/; 01-market-report.md). Plan on the low-to-mid column, and treat anything above about $12k/yr in year one as unlikely.

## 4. Ranking

**Score = SAM(mid) × price × reachability** (normalised so top = 100). Reachability is my 1-5 judgement [estimate]: 5 for realtors and teachers (large, active Etsy/TPT/Pinterest/Facebook-group buying culture, many named-niche shops already exist per 04-competition-check), 4 for nurses, SLPs and homeschool, 3 for the rest.

1. **Freelancers / self-employed**: 100. Wins on pool size and a $25 price. Weakness: the 27.6M pool is mostly non-knowledge workers, template buying is unproven, and tool-spend data is vendor blog data.
2. **Real-estate agents**: 42. Highest price ($30), income and expense data all [primary], 87% independent contractors, tools already in daily use.
3. **K-12 teachers**: 19. Biggest evidence of repeat purchasing and willingness to spend their own money ($895), but low prices ($5) and the most crowded marketplace.
4. Homeschool: 9. 5. Nurses: 4. 6-10. Tutors/coaches, SLP, ABA, daycare, OT: 1-2 each.

**Sensitivity:** halve the freelancer buy share and drop its reachability from 3 to 2, and its score falls to about 33, putting realtors first. These are within the error of my assumptions.

## 5. Which has the best justification

**Real-estate agents.** It is not the top model score, but it is the best-evidenced pick: the headcount, income, expenses and independent-contractor status are all from NAR [primary]; the $30 price makes each sale worth about six teacher sales; and it is a single, nameable audience that can be reached by niche keywords. Freelancers have the biggest paper market but the weakest evidence that they buy templates. Teachers have the best repeat-buying evidence but the lowest price and heaviest competition (the TPT numbers show a winner-take-most market). Therapist audiences (SLP, OT, ABA) have good headcounts and $12 pricing but no spending evidence and small pools; they are a niche add-on, not a first bet.

## 6. What is not verified
- No survey found on nurse, SLP, OT, ABA, daycare or tutor/coach spending on digital templates. Buy shares for those are guesses.
- BLS and NCES pages were not directly readable; numbers came from search snippets.
- US-only tutors/coaches count not found (ICF figure is worldwide).
- Buy share, price per order, orders per year, capture share and reachability scores are all my assumptions. Validate with a real listing test (10 listings, 30 days) before committing.
