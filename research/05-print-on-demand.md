# Print-on-demand (POD): t-shirts, caps, mugs, coloring books

_2026-10-03. Sources are blog/vendor claims from search summaries (no primary pages reachable). Treat numbers as ranges._

## Model
We create the design; a partner (Printful, Printify, Gelato, Amazon Merch, KDP Print) prints, ships and handles returns when someone orders. No inventory. Unlike digital downloads, each sale has a **per-unit cost**, so margins are lower.

## Market
- POD market ~$12.96B (2025) growing ~25%/yr; apparel is ~39.7% of it [blog-claim].
- Typical POD margins 20–40%; t-shirts $3–8 net per shirt after costs, 10–50% depending on price and ad spend [blog-claim].
- Typical earnings claims: median active Etsy POD seller $500–1,500/mo; 100+ designs and active optimizing $1,000–3,000/mo; top 10% $5k–20k+ [blog-claim, likely optimistic].
- Kids coloring/activity books: Circana counted 16.7M US units in 2025 (+18%), still +18% through May 2026 [reported]. But 100k+ active coloring titles on Amazon, so niche positioning is required.

## Platforms & rules
| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| Etsy + Printify/Printful | Allowed if you are the original designer, disclose the production partner, label "Designed by a seller" (June 2025 rules). Generic designs get buried |
| Amazon Merch on Demand | No upfront cost. From June 1 2026 royalty tiers: Creator ~$2.44 per $19.99 tee (Amazon-organic traffic), Plus ~$4.88 (15%+ outside traffic), Premium ~$5.27 (35%+ outside traffic) [blog-claim]. Organic-only sellers saw a ~50% royalty cut. Approval required |
| KDP coloring books | Zero upfront; royalty ~$2–5 per book; 100-page single-sided interiors, niche themes, finished-art covers |
| Redbubble/Teespring etc. | Easy to start, low per-sale profit, heavy competition |

## Digital vs POD
| | Digital downloads | POD |
|---|---|---|
| Margin per sale | ~85–95% | ~20–50% (about $3–8 per tee) |
| Per-unit cost | none | yes |
| Returns/shipping issues | none | yes (partner handles, but quality complaints land on you) |
| Market size | big | bigger, but more crowded |
| IP risk | moderate | high: trademark, character and slogan takedowns |

## Verdict
POD is a real, large market but harder money per hour: low margin, saturated generic designs, trademark risk, and it needs traffic (ads/Pinterest/TikTok). Best entry for us: **coloring books** (we already have the KDP pipeline, no inventory, no shipping) and **niche apparel** with original designs for one tight audience (e.g. nurses, using the Shiftwise audience). Printable coloring pages as digital downloads give the high margin with the same artwork.
