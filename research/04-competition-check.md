## Honest verdict
- **Sellable as products:** yes, all five are real, usable products and the categories have paying customers.
- **Competitive today:** none is clearly better than what is already listed. They are **entry-level competitive**, not standouts.
- **Best chances:** freelancer trackers (differentiate with bundle + mileage/deduction + Notion) and the realtor kit (needs to grow to 25–40 pages and add a seller deck).
- **Weakest:** TPT fractions (low prices, huge bundles) and the KDP journal as-is (near-copy structure of existing titles).
- Nothing is proven until a real Etsy/KDP/TPT search shows sales and review counts for these competitors.
