# Market Report #1 – What digital products sell, and what they earn

_Date: 2026-10-03. Method: web research. **Caveat:** most income numbers come from seller/affiliate blogs, which skew optimistic. Treat them as ranges, not promises. Only Etsy's marketplace totals come from a primary source (its 10-K)._

## 1. Platform snapshot

| Platform | Scale / fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | 2025 marketplace GMS ≈ $10.46B, 86.5M active buyers ([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm)). Fees: $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + ~3% + $0.25 payment; Offsite Ads 12–15% ([source](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees)) | Digital downloads are a top category; ~4.5M listings, saturated sub-niches ([source](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026)) |
| **Amazon KDP** | Royalty-based; no upfront cost | Low-content books getting more competitive; KDP removes near-duplicate/low-quality titles ([source](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)) |
| **Gumroad / Payhip** | Gumroad ~10% + processing; Payhip ~5% ([source](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/gumroad-vs-lemon-squeezy-vs-payhip-for-selling-digital-templates-2025)) | You supply the traffic; no built-in marketplace demand like Etsy |
| **Teachers Pay Teachers** | 7M+ products, 100k+ active sellers; ~12 millionaires, ~300 sellers >$100k ([source](https://trtc.io/blog/details/Teachers-Pay-Teachers)) | Very concentrated: ~half of sellers earn <$20/mo |

## 2. Product categories with proven demand

1. **Editable Canva templates** (social media, niche professions, wedding/event) – listed among the top sellers on Etsy ([source](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)).
2. **Digital planners / trackers** (annual planners, budget & habit trackers; Google Sheets tools) – some planner listings reportedly passed 20,000 sales.
3. **Printable wall art & posters** – described as one of the highest-volume digital categories; one educational poster reportedly 161k+ sales.
4. **SVG / PNG / clipart / cut files** – consistently outsell most categories on Etsy.
5. **Classroom worksheets & teaching resources** – TPT + Etsy.
6. **Notion templates** – dominate the productivity niche; typical price $15–47 ([source](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/best-platform-for-selling-digital-downloads-15-creator-case-studies)).
7. **KDP guided journals, puzzle books, coloring books, logbooks** – medium-content (more than blank pages) is the stronger direction.
8. **PLR/MRR bundles** – reported $2k+/week for some stores, but legally/ethically murkier and highly saturated; deprioritized.

## 3. How much money?

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| Etsy digital sellers | Median ~£400–800/mo; top 10% £8k–30k+/mo with 50–200 optimized listings ([source](https://www.nichepursuits.com/best-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)) |
| Margins | ~90–95% after Etsy fees for digital goods (before ads) |
| Templates (Gumroad etc.) | Established sellers with audience: $5k–20k/mo; avg price $15–47 |
| KDP low content | 1–3 books: <$100/mo; 5–10 books: meaningful income; niches $200–1,200+/mo ([source](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/low-content-books-income/)) |
| TPT | Top ~1% earn $100k+/yr; long tail earns almost nothing |

**Realistic read:** income follows a power law. Expect most shops to earn little in the first 3–6 months; winners come from tight niches, 50+ listings, and bundles. Plan for a portfolio of small bets, not one hit.

## 4. Risks
- **Etsy AI policy:** AI-assisted products are allowed if disclosed ("Designed by seller"); 17,000+ listings were reportedly removed in early 2025 for disclosure problems ([source](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)). We must disclose and add real human design input.
- **Saturation:** generic planners/wall art are crowded; niche down.
- **Platform dependency:** fees and policy changes; plan to also sell via own Stripe/Payhip checkout.
- **IP:** no trademarked characters, brands, or copied designs.

## 5. Recommended first three businesses
(Details and scores in `opportunities.csv`.)

1. **Niche Canva template shop** – e.g. templates for one profession (realtors, therapists, bakers). Low cost, repeatable, proven on Etsy.
2. **Notion / Google Sheets trackers for one audience** (freelancers, ADHD, small business finance). Higher price point ($15–47), less saturated than printables.
3. **Teacher resources in one grade/subject** (bundles). Strong buyer intent, bundle-friendly; slow to start.

KDP guided journals are a good 4th once we have a design workflow.
