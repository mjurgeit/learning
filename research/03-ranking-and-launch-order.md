## Recommended order
1. **Weeks 1–2: Solo Ledger (trackers).** Open each .xlsx in real Excel/Google Sheets, validate keywords in Etsy search/eRank, list the first 5 products.
2. **Weeks 3–4: Realtor Canva kit** (build the Canva templates from the specs; needs a Canva Pro account and a compliance review of any contract-like language).
3. **Month 2: Shiftwise planners**, once the gap is validated in Etsy search.
4. KDP and TPT only after 1–3 show traction.

## What I (Claude) can and can't do
- Can: write plans, listing copy, generate PDFs/spreadsheets/mockups, run the toolkit, draft emails/Pinterest pins, analyze your sales exports.
- Can't: create your Etsy/Payhip/KDP/TPT accounts, pass identity/payout verification, or log in to upload (human steps), and can't see live Etsy data from here.

## Must-verify before spending money
- Official fee pages (Etsy, TPT, KDP royalty/print cost); the playbook used secondary sources only.
- Competitor sales/review counts and keyword volumes in a real browser.
- Tax/legal items (sales tax/VAT, tax-advice disclaimers, real-estate compliance): consult a professional.
- Open all spreadsheets in real Excel and Google Sheets; check KDP interiors in KDP Previewer.
