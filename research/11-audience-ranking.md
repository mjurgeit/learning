## Pattern
The best audiences are **small-business owners with a state-regulated compliance pain** (licensing, audits, notices), **annual updates** and **accuracy that a generic chatbot can't guarantee**. They pay $79–$399 per product, not $3–6. This overrides the earlier realtor/freelancer preference in report 10.

## Catch (important)
These niches sell *accuracy*. State-specific legal or licensing content must come from official sources and ideally a professional's review. Claude can draft, but cannot certify legal correctness. Use "template, not legal advice" disclaimers, label state + date, and avoid "compliant/guaranteed" claims. For health-related audiences keep products PHI-free.

## Recommended first tests (one state each, cap ~$100 + review cost)
1. **Home-care agency policy-and-procedure manual** for one state (price evidence $149–$399).
2. **Landlord state notice pack** for one state ($19–39 per state in the agent's ideas; legal-risk review required).
3. Runner-up: daycare state licensing handbook kit.
Pass/fail: ≥5 paid orders or ≥25 qualified signups in 30 days.

## Verify first
- Official state statutes/licensing pages (not blogs) for the chosen state.
- Competitor price/sales in a real browser.
- Headcounts (landlords, home-care agencies) from Census/state licensing lists.
