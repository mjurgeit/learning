(3) Offsite Ads is mandatory above a revenue threshold (check current Etsy rules) and costs 12-15% per attributed sale; price for it. (4) Etsy-in-ChatGPT is a new, tiny channel; its listing-quality and keyword relevance rules are unverified.

## 2. Etsy search volume: methodology and public numbers

- Etsy publishes no search-volume counts. eRank, Marmalead, EverBee, RankHero, Alura and similar tools model volume from clickstream panels, Google data and Etsy autocomplete. Their numbers disagree with each other. [estimate]
- RankHero pages state that trend changes are measured between two months a year apart (for example "rose 22% between March 2025 and February 2026"). Treat each as a point-to-point comparison, not a smoothed trend. [estimate]
- Seasonality is the main trap. "2027 planner" up 3,900% is a pre-season ramp, not a new niche.

- Pinterest is a search engine for planning; for a digital seller it drives traffic to your own page or Etsy, which is the external traffic Etsy rewards poorly in its own stats (section 1: social <5% in the one shop breakdown). Treat it as a slow compounding channel.

## 4. TikTok / BookTok and KDP

- ~59M print book sales in 2024 tied to BookTok content [blog-claim, attributed to Publishers Weekly] https://publishingperspectives.com/tag/booktok/
- Meltwater: ~1.3M BookTok mentions and 1.7B engagements, 1 Jan-21 Jun 2026, increasingly romance- and title-driven fandoms. [estimate, social-listening] https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/booktok-trends-social-listening-analysis
- BookTok sells fiction and bestsellers. I found no data showing BookTok drives low-content KDP (planners, logbooks, journals). Do not assume it does.
- KDP low/medium content: Amazon increased scrutiny and removes duplicative/low-quality titles; "blank notebooks and simple planners" era described as over; medium-content books favored. [blog-claim] https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon and https://the-write-moves.beehiiv.com/p/amazon-s-algorithm-tweak-of-2025-winners-and-losers-in-low-content-listings
- Self-published titles topped 3.5M in 2025 (Bowker via Publishers Weekly). [blog-claim, secondhand] https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12351

## 5. Amazon share of product discovery

- Jungle Scout 2024 survey of 1,000 US online shoppers: 56% start product searches on Amazon, 42% on search engines, 29% Walmart.com; down from 61% in 2022. [survey] (search summary; Jungle Scout consumer trends report, exact URL not opened)
- Feedvisor: 66% begin on Amazon. [survey, older, sample not checked] https://feedvisor.com/resources/amazon-trends/amazon-expert-briefing-what-powers-purchase-decisions-from-2000-u-s-consumers/
- These measure physical-product shopping. For KDP, it means Amazon on-site search is the dominant discovery path, so keywords in title/subtitle/backend matter more than social.

## 8. What this means for the lab (inference, [estimate])

1. Plan for Etsy-internal search as the main channel; budget Offsite Ads at 12-15% of sale price in margin.
2. Prioritize specific, dated or problem-framed digital products (2027 planners, weekly reset, habit trackers, ADHD-specific tools) over generic "digital planner", SVG, or AI wall art.
3. Treat Pinterest as a free long-run supplementary channel, and the KDP BookTok route as relevant to fiction only.
4. Check seasonality before launching: planners peak January, listings need to be live and aging by Oct-Nov.

## 9. How to verify each claim in a real browser

Free tiers and steps (cannot be done from this sandbox; do them on your own machine):

1. **Etsy 10-K / shareholder letters.** Open https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1370637&type=10-K . Open the FY2025 10-K, press Ctrl+F for "Paid GMS", "Offsite Ads", "app", "Marketing". For Q2 2026 open the 8-K Exhibit 99 shareholder letter (URL in section 1) and search "paid search", "habitual", "agentic". Free.
2. **Etsy traffic sources for your own shop.** Etsy Shop Manager > Stats > Traffic sources. Compare Etsy Search, Etsy app, Direct, Social, Etsy Ads. Free; only meaningful after a few hundred visits.
3. **Etsy Similarweb claims.** Go to https://www.similarweb.com/website/etsy.com/ . The free view shows the traffic-source split (direct, search, social, referral) for the last month. Compare with the 43% claim.
4. **eRank.** Free account at https://erank.com (limited searches/day). Keyword Tool > enter term > read search volume, competition, trend graph. Check 2027 planner, weekly reset planner, daily habit tracker, adhd notepad. Blog trend reports at https://help.erank.com/blog/ (free to read).
5. **RankHero.** Open the exact page, for example https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/2027-planner . It shows volume, listing count, difficulty and the "rose/dropped X% between [months]" sentence I quoted. No login needed for these pages (as far as the search results indicated; confirm).
6. **Marmalead / EverBee / Alura.** Marmalead and EverBee have free tiers or trials with capped lookups; Alura has a limited free plan. Use them to cross-check the same 10 keywords. If two tools disagree on direction, treat the claim as unconfirmed.
7. **Etsy's own search.** Type the term into etsy.com, read the autocomplete order (a rough popularity signal), and note the "results" count (listing competition). Filter to "Digital download" and sort by "Most recent" to see how crowded fresh listings are.
8. **Google Trends.** https://trends.google.com/trends/ : compare terms over 5 years, region US, Shopping or Web search. Check the 2027 planner pattern by comparing the "2026 planner" curve over Oct 2025-Jan 2026. Free.
9. **Pinterest Trends.** https://trends.pinterest.com/ (free, needs a Pinterest account). Search "digital planner", "printable", "teacher planner", "habit tracker", "career planning", "home office", "wellness journal"; set region US, time 12 months and 3 years; read the weekly curve and the "growing" label. Download the Predicts report from https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/ and check which trends Pinterest names.
10. **BookTok.** Open TikTok, search #booktok and #kdp; check Circana BookScan via Publishers Weekly articles for sales attribution. For KDP demand, use Amazon search autocomplete and the Best Sellers Rank on product pages, not TikTok view counts.
11. **Amazon discovery claims.** Search "Jungle Scout consumer trends report" and open the PDF to find the 56% number and its sample, or run your own check by listing your KDP book and watching Amazon search vs external traffic in KDP reports.
12. **Declining claims.** For each keyword in section 7, open the RankHero page and the Google Trends graph; if both show a decline, treat as real. For listing counts, search the term on Etsy and read the result count.
